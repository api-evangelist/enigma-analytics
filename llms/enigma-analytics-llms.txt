# Enigma Analytics

> Enigma (Enigma Technologies) operates a knowledge graph of every U.S. business — 100M+ business
> registrations and 2B+ relationships across brands, legal entities, operating locations, addresses,
> and people. It is exposed through a Relay-style GraphQL API, a hosted Console, and a remote MCP
> server, powering KYB onboarding, sanctions/watchlist screening, negative-news and government-archive
> checks, TIN verification, card-transaction analytics, and payment underwriting and GTM signals.
> Generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the Enigma developer surface.

## APIs
- [Enigma GraphQL API](https://documentation.enigma.com/reference/graphql_api/): Relay-style GraphQL over the business knowledge graph; endpoint https://api.enigma.com/graphql, x-api-key header auth.

## Agent access (MCP)
- [Enigma MCP server](https://documentation.enigma.com/guides/ai-mcp): remote MCP at https://mcp.enigma.com/mcp (streamable HTTP, bearer auth) exposing 14 tools — search_business, search_person, get_brand_locations, get_brand_card_analytics, get_brand_legal_entities, search_negative_news, search_gov_archive, search_kyb, screen_customer, screen_business, screen_entity_search, find_decision, find_decisions, update_decision.
