AI agents are increasingly asked to verify businesses with data that just doesn't live in their model. Primary state filings, beneficial-ownership submissions, professional licenses, sanctions records: all of it lives in primary sources the model has never seen, changing on cadences model retraining can't track. This post walks through the reference layer we've been building for every KYB workflow, and looks at three ways of delivering that layer to agents in the field: our MCP server, our KYB Compliance Agent on Google Cloud, and Parallel's Index, the new model for compensating data providers
