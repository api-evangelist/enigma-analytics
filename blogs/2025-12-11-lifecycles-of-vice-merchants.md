Enigma analyzes 853,000+ U.S. vice business registrations to reveal why some alcohol, tobacco, and gambling businesses endure while others fail quickly. The article shows that business model structure—capital intensity, distribution roles, and regulatory moats—matters far more for survival than the simple fact of selling “recession-proof” vice products.
