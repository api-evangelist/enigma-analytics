Enigma’s analysis reveals that America’s “holiday season” isn’t a single tidal wave—it’s a cycle with winners, losers, and an inevitable reset. Drawing on transaction data from 410,001 holiday-sensitive businesses across 105 industries (2020–2024), the piece shows how December delivers dramatic surges for gift-driven retailers like electronics, jewelry, and specialty shops, even as hospitality and entertainment often slump as consumers stay close to home.
